# Nguồn hình ảnh Quán Phở

Nền `quan/quan-pho.webp` được tạo cho Bếp Phố bằng ImageGen tích hợp, sau đó chuyển sang WebP. Bàn, khách và nhân viên được vẽ trên Canvas theo trạng thái game.

## Prompt nền quán

> Create a single original 2D game environment background plate for a cozy Vietnamese phở restaurant management web game. LANDSCAPE 4:3 composition. Illustrative hand-painted 2D game art, crisp softly outlined forms, soft paper texture, warm apricot and amber lacquer, desaturated jade plants, reddish brick, warm daylight. Oblique overhead dollhouse view with lines nearly horizontal across the scene (not a rotated diamond), simple readable comic perspective. The visible cafe fills the frame edge-to-edge. Top 25 percent: back wall with warm ochre plaster, cream and dark green checker dado tile, a Vietnamese stainless stockpot cooking station LEFT at x=23%, serving counter with little jars and bowls to the RIGHT at x=75%, shelving, centered small open dark warm doorway to back utility room. No lettering anywhere. Left and right edges may have tall wooden windows, terracotta plants, hanging amber lamps with painted glow. Main central area from x=12% to 88%, y=35% to 84% is completely EMPTY open warm peach and sandstone checker floor for furniture and sprites added by the game later, low contrast, roomy, no objects on the floor there. At bottom edge y=89-100%, a worn red brick sidewalk seen from above, clear center opening (shop entrance), narrow plants at outer corners. One coherent painting with sunlight on tiled floor and subtle texture. Saturated enough to feel welcoming with dark brown accents; no white margin, no HUD, no boxes or text, no tables or chairs, no people, no graphics, no logos. Original Vietnamese family-run neighborhood eatery; designed as art asset for an interactive game, not a screenshot of another game.
